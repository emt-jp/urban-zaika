## A. 忘年会 and company catering — the nearest money

Tokyo offices book year-end parties in October. The pitch is not "Indian food"; it is **"nobody
on your team has to sit this one out"** — the halal and vegetarian angle solves a real problem
for the person organising it.

Deliver by hand to offices near either branch, or email where a contact form exists.

**件名：忘年会・社内イベントのケータリングのご案内（ハラール認証／ベジタリアン対応）**

> 突然のご連絡失礼いたします。東京都北区上十条と江戸川区上一色でインド・パキスタン料理店を営んでおります Urban Zaika と申します。
>
> 忘年会や社内のご会食で、宗教上の理由やベジタリアンの方がいらっしゃるために店選びに苦労された経験はございませんでしょうか。当店は両店舗ともハラール認証を取得したキッチンで調理しており、ベジタリアン料理もご用意しています。どなたも同じ食卓を囲んでいただけます。
>
> ・ビリヤニ、カラヒ、バターチキン、焼きたてナン、ケバブ など
> ・30〜100名様程度まで対応（それ以上は2店舗で分担いたします）
> ・当日のご注文にも対応できる場合がございます
> ・北区・江戸川区を中心に、周辺の区へお届けします
>
> ご人数と日時をお知らせいただければ、お見積りをお出しいたします。ご検討いただけますと幸いです。

**English version, for international offices**

> I hope you don't mind the cold approach. We run Urban Zaika, an Indian and Pakistani restaurant
> with branches in Kita-ku and Edogawa-ku.
>
> If your team has ever struggled to book a year-end party because someone can't eat what's being
> served, we may be able to help. Both our kitchens are halal certified and we cook a full
> vegetarian selection, so nobody has to sit the meal out.
>
> We cater 30–100 guests from one kitchen, more across both with notice, and can often cook the
> same day. Biryani, karahi, butter chicken, fresh naan and kebabs.
>
> Tell us numbers and a date and we'll send a quote.

## B. Mosque committees — the repeat business

Ask for the committee, not the imam. Committees book the same caterer again and again once they
trust one, and Ramadan is decided months ahead. **Go in person** where you can — these are the
places mapped in `community-outreach.md` §5, starting with Madina Masjid in Higashi-Jujo, a few
minutes from the Jujo branch.

**English**

> Assalamu alaikum.
>
> My name is [name] and I run Urban Zaika, a halal Indian and Pakistani restaurant with two
> branches in Tokyo — one in Kami-Jujo, Kita-ku, very close to this masjid, and one in
> Kamiisshiki, Edogawa-ku.
>
> Both our kitchens are halal certified. I wanted to introduce myself and ask whether the
> committee has a caterer for iftar, Eid and community events. We cook biryani, karahi, nihari,
> kebabs and fresh naan, and we can serve 30–100 people from one kitchen, more with notice.
>
> If it would help, I'd be glad to bring food for the committee to try, at no cost, so you can
> judge it yourselves before Ramadan planning starts.
>
> Jazakallahu khairan.

## D. Festival organisers — for 2027 pitches

Send now. The 2026 windows have closed, but these lists are built early and being known to the
organiser before applications open matters more than the application itself.

**To the Pakistan Japan Friendship Festival** (pjftokyo.com contact form, "ご出店について")

**件名：出店についてのお問い合わせ（ハラール認証 インド・パキスタン料理）**

> はじめまして。東京都北区上十条および江戸川区上一色でハラール認証のインド・パキスタン料理店を営んでおります Urban Zaika と申します。
>
> 次回のフェスティバルへの出店を希望しております。ハイデラバード式ビリヤニ、ラホール風カラヒ、シークカバブ、焼きたてナンを提供しております。両店舗ともハラール認証を取得したキッチンです。
>
> 出店の募集時期、参加条件、出店料についてご教示いただけますでしょうか。また、募集開始の際にご連絡をいただけますと大変ありがたく存じます。
>
> どうぞよろしくお願いいたします。

**English, same message** — the organisers work in both:

> We are Urban Zaika, a halal-certified Indian and Pakistani restaurant with branches in Kita-ku
> and Edogawa-ku, Tokyo. We would like to apply for a food stall at the next festival —

**To the Japan Bangladesh Society** for Boishakhi Mela (mid-April, Ikebukuro) — same text, with

> Bangladeshi community in Higashi-Jujo.

Same again for the **Nepal Festival** (NRNA Japan, July, Odaiba), with the halal emphasis dropped
and vegetarian range mentioned instead — that audience is largely Hindu.

## E. Embassies — the large bookings

Ask for the community affairs or cultural section. These are slow, formal, and worth a single
well-written letter rather than repeated chasing. Pakistan is in Moto-Azabu, Bangladesh in
Kioicho, Malaysia in Nanpeidai-cho, Indonesia in Higashi-Gotanda, Nepal in Shimo-Meguro.

> Dear [Community Affairs / Cultural Section],
>
> We are Urban Zaika, a halal-certified Indian and Pakistani restaurant operating two kitchens in
> Tokyo, in Kita-ku and Edogawa-ku.
>
> We cater community events — national day gatherings, iftar, Eid, and cultural functions — and
> would be glad to be considered for the Embassy's events or to be added to your list of
> suppliers. We cook Hyderabadi biryani, karahi, nihari, tandoori grills and a full vegetarian
> selection, and can serve 30–100 guests from one kitchen and more across both with notice.
> English, Urdu and Bengali are spoken by our staff.
>
> We would be happy to provide a tasting at our own cost at a time that suits you.

## Keeping track

Every one of these is worth recording: who, when contacted, what they said, when to follow up.
A missed follow-up is the usual reason cold outreach produces nothing — most of these will need a
second contact before they answer.
