## The eight shots that carry the month

| # | Shot | How to film |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Biryani pot opening** | Camera low, lid lifts, steam rises, spoon digs to show meat buried in the rice. Hold 3 seconds on the lift. |
| 2 | **Naan into the tandoor** | Hand slaps dough on the wall, cut, then the puffed naan pulled out with the hook. |
| 3 | **Karahi on the flame** | Close on the wok, tomato and butter going in, the stir, the sizzle. Sound matters — don't add music over it. |
| 4 | **Kebabs on the grill** | Seekh kebabs turning, fat dripping, smoke. Golden hour if possible. |
| 5 | **Lunch set build** | Empty tray → curry, naan, salad, drink placed one by one. Ends on the ¥650 price card. |
| 6 | **Cheese naan pull** | Tear it slowly, cheese stretches. This one travels furthest on TikTok. |
| 7 | **Mango lassi pour** | Slow pour into the glass. A reviewer called it the best they'd had in Japan — say so on screen. |
| 8 | **Delivery bag pack** | Containers sealed, bag stapled, handed over. Caption points to the direct-order number. |

Film all eight in one session, roughly an hour. That's the month covered.

## Caption rules

- Japanese first, English second, no more than two short lines each.
- Always name the branch: 上一色店 or 十条店.
- Geotag the branch every time.
- Put the phone number in the caption on any post about delivery.
- Reply to every comment within a day — reach rewards it.

Lunch crowd 11:00–11:30, dinner crowd 17:30–18:30. Those are the windows when people are deciding what to eat.

## Mosque and community noticeboards

Tokyo Camii, Otsuka Mosque, Asakusa Mosque, and the prayer rooms near Kita-ku — plus halal grocery shops, which usually allow a flyer. The A5 flyers from `scripts/generate-order-flyers.py` work as-is; ask for the poster variant if you want A4.

**Ramadan starts around February 2027.** Iftar sets and group packs get booked weeks ahead — that's the biggest single window of the year for a halal restaurant, and it needs planning by December.
